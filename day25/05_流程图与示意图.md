## 3. 单轮对话时序

```sequenceDiagram
    participant U as 用户
    participant R as REPL
    participant S as LangChainSession
    participant P as ChatPromptTemplate
    participant L as ChatModel

    U->>R: 输入文本
    R->>S: add_user()
    R->>P: format_messages(history, user_input)
    P->>L: invoke(messages)
    L-->>R: AIMessage
    R->>S: add_assistant()
    R-->>U: 打印助手回复
```
